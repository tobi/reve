# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, disagree with ideas rather than people, and give
constructive feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is harmful to the community. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, private where possible, and focused on restoring a safe environment.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through
<https://github.com/tobi/leve/security/advisories/new>. Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public issue.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing
the project.
